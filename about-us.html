What is Study Companion?

Study Companion is an independent educational website designed to bring study resources and learning tools together in one place.

What you can do

Depending on availability, visitors can browse textbooks and study materials, access previous-year question papers, read PDFs, use study utilities and ask StudyMate AI for explanations.

Our approach

The goal is to support understanding, revision and independent learning. AI answers are intended as learning assistance and should be checked against trusted educational sources.
