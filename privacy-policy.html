Privacy Policy

Study Companion · Information and policies

Overview

Study Companion is an educational website that provides study materials, PDF access and StudyMate AI features. This page explains what information may be processed when you use the website.

Information you provide

If you create an account or sign in, Firebase Authentication processes the account information needed for sign-in. If you choose to save a profile, details you enter may be stored in the Study Companion Firebase Realtime Database under your account. Do not enter information you do not want stored.

StudyMate AI messages

When you send a question, voice transcription or attached study text to StudyMate AI, the content is sent to the configured AI service through the website's AI endpoint so that it can generate a response. Avoid sending passwords, financial details, private documents or sensitive personal information.

Device storage

The website may store preferences, saved conversations, PDF annotations and other app settings in your browser's local storage. These items may remain on your device until you clear browser data or remove them using available app controls.

Third-party services

The website uses Google/Firebase services for authentication and database features, Google Drive for some PDF resources, and Google AdSense advertising code. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies and terms. Their handling of data is not controlled by this website.

Advertising and analytics

An AdSense script and ad placement are present in the website. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies subject to your settings and applicable consent requirements. The Firebase project also contains an analytics measurement identifier; analytics collection should be considered enabled only if the relevant analytics code is actually activated.

Children and student users

This is an educational website that may be used by students. Students should use it with appropriate parent or guardian guidance and should not submit sensitive personal information.

Your choices and contact
